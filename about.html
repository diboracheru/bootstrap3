About Starbucks

Inspiring and nurturing the human spirit, one cup at a time.

Who We Are

Starbucks is a global coffee company that offers coffee, handcrafted beverages, and a welcoming environment for customers.

We believe that coffee is more than just a drink. It is an opportunity to connect with people and create memorable moments.

Our stores provide a comfortable place where customers can relax and enjoy their favorite beverages.

Our Story

Starbucks was founded in 1971 in Seattle, Washington, USA.

It started as a small store selling coffee beans and equipment.

Over the years, Starbucks grew into one of the most recognized coffee companies in the world.
